Queue Blocks

A Spotify queue made of blocks. Blocks wait on the shelf, shrink as they play in the queue, and disappear when they run out.

Things to try
Tap a block header to fold or unfold its songs.
Drag a shelf block into the queue. A gap opens where it will land; drop it on what's playing to swap.
Drop a block between two songs of an open block to merge it in there.
Drag a song's handle between songs, into another block, or down to the shelf.
Tap Back to return to the last song. Played sits above Now playing.
Tap Shelf to open the whole tray. Last 24 hours lists every song you queued.
+ in the queue header or the Search tab: recommended, frequently queued, then search. Adding asks for a block, or Just add to queue.
