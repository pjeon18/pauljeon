Queue Blocks

Four ways to show one model. Blocks hold their place while they wait, shrink as they play, and disappear when they run out.

Concepts
Playback
Time
Rules
Finished songs leave the block. Skipped songs leave too.
On a block's last song, you're offered a copy of the original.
Queued today keeps every song you queued. It clears at midnight.
Blocks left on the shelf for two weeks get a cleanup prompt.
